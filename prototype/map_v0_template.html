    How to read this

    Interior lots is the lowest value BIR sets in the barangay, usually "all other streets". Prime frontage is the highest, usually a main road or a named condo. Typical is the geometric midpoint of the two, a rough stand-in for a median lot.

    Hatched areas have no barangay detail yet and show the city median instead: all of Caloocan, plus a handful of barangays elsewhere. Manila and every city outside NCR are shown at city level for now. Grey means not pulled yet.

    Schedules are stale. Effectivity dates run from 2018 (Bulacan) to 2024 (QC, south Laguna). A 2018 value and a 2024 value are not comparable, which is why Bulacan looks cheaper than it trades.

    Data: BIR zonal value schedules as indexed by REN.PH, pulled Oct 2026. Boundaries: PSA/NAMRIA via faeldon/philippines-json-maps (PSGC Dec 2023). Verify with the BIR RDO before any transaction.
