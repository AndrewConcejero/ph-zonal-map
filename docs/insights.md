# Places where BIR values look low for what they offer

A screening, not advice. Values are BIR zonal values (tax floors), adjusted to today with each district's own revision growth. "Cheaper than predicted" compares a place with places that have the same flood hazard, surface heat, distance to rail and expressway, and distance to the nearest business district (Makati CBD, BGC, Ortigas, Alabang, Binondo/Manila, Eastwood/Cubao). Shortlists also require: flood hazard (medium or high, 100-year rain) on 10% of the area or less, surface heat at or below the median (47 °C), and a rail station or expressway within 2 km. Everything else that sets prices (schools, security, lot sizes, title and zoning issues) is not in the model, so verify on the ground and with the BIR Revenue District Office.

Model fit on 3,130 barangays. How values move with each factor, holding the others fixed. These are associations, not prices of risk: central, low-lying, dense areas are both hotter and dearer, so flood and heat come out positive. That is why they are used as filters below rather than trusted as discounts.
