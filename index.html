What the taxman thinks Metro Manila land is worth

Barangay by barangay across Metro Manila, Bulacan, Pampanga, Zambales, Rizal, Cavite, Laguna, Batangas and Quezon. These are BIR tax floors, not market prices, so read the map as a ranking.

      How to read this

      Zonal values are BIR tax floors, not market prices. BIR uses them to compute taxes on property sales. Market prices usually run higher, sometimes by a lot. Use the map to see which places are valued above others.

      Interior lot is the lowest residential land value BIR sets in the barangay, usually for "all other streets". Prime is the highest, usually a main road or a named subdivision. Typical is the median of the barangay's street values, each street counted once. Condominium values are left out: BIR prices those per square meter of floor area, not land.

      Residential or commercial. BIR sets separate values for residential land (class RR), commercial land (CR, usually higher along main roads) and industrial land (I). The toggle switches every view between them, including the villages, streets and condominiums shown when zoomed in.

      Zooming in on Metro Manila shows BIR's values for individual villages and streets, matched to OpenStreetMap shapes. Up close, a barangay's colour becomes its interior-lot value, what BIR charges on streets it does not list.

      Solid colour means the barangay has its own BIR values. Hatched and faded means it does not: it shows an estimate (the median of neighbouring barangays) or, where no neighbour has values, the city median. Grey means no data yet.

      Schedules are stale, unevenly. BIR revises each Revenue District Office on its own timetable, so most schedules in force date from 2018 to 2024, and a 2018 value is not comparable with a 2024 one. Adjusted to today grows each value from its schedule's effectivity date at its district's own pace: the median yearly increase BIR gave the same streets between consecutive revisions. It is a projection of BIR's own trend, not a market estimate; the card shows both numbers and the rate used.

      Colour by swaps land value for another factor: share of the barangay in medium or high flood hazard (100-year rain), average elevation (a surface model, so towers add height), dry-season afternoon surface temperature (roofs and roads, not air temperature), and distance to the nearest rail station or expressway. Condos marks buildings with BIR's condominium value, per square metre of floor area. Roads & rail shows expressways and rail, dashed where under construction and dotted where proposed.

      Before any transaction, verify the zonal value with the BIR Revenue District Office that covers the property. This site can be wrong or out of date.
